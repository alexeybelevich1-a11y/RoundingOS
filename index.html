Settings & backup

All data stays on this device, encrypted with your passphrase.

Backup

Discharged patients

Only patients you mark "Discharged" (Actions menu) are archived. "Discharge today" never is. Archived patients leave the census and stay under the Archive filter, where you can return them. After the set time they are deleted from this device for good, including from saved copies. Export a backup first if you need to keep them.

    Import backup

    Merge keeps the newer copy of each patient. Replace everything swaps your census and settings for the backup. A copy of your current data is kept so you can undo either one.

    Saved copies

    Copies of all your data, taken once a day and before an import, a restore or clearing the census. The last 7 are kept on this device.
